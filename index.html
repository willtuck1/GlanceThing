GlanceThing

Your calendar, to-dos and sports scores on a Spotify Car Thing.

GlanceThing is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux. It turns a Spotify Car Thing into a small glanceable display with four tabs:

  • Calendar: your upcoming Google Calendar events for the next 7 days.
  • To-do: your Google Tasks, which you can tick off from the device.
  • Sports: live NBA and NFL scores, with favorite teams.
  • Spotify: the original GlanceThing playback controls.

How it uses your Google account

When you click Connect in the desktop app's Settings, GlanceThing asks Google for:

  • See your calendars and events (read-only), to show upcoming events on the Car Thing.
  • Create, edit, organize and delete your tasks, to show your to-do list and mark tasks done or not done when you tick them on the device.

Everything runs on your own computer. GlanceThing has no server: your data goes only from Google to the app on your computer and, over USB, to your Car Thing. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
