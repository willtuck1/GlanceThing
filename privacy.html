Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers the GlanceThing desktop app published at github.com/willtuck1/GlanceThing ("GlanceThing", "the app") and its Car Thing client.

Summary

GlanceThing runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives your Google data.

Google user data the app accesses

If you connect a Google account, the app requests these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: to read your calendar list and the events in the calendars you select, for the next 7 days.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks: to read your task lists and tasks, and to change a task's status between completed and not completed when you tick it on the Car Thing.

How that data is used

  • Event titles, times and locations, and task titles, due dates and status, are shown on your Car Thing.
  • The app changes a task's completed status only when you tick or untick it on the device. It does not create, edit or delete events, and does not delete tasks.
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI or machine-learning models, and is not sold.

Where data is stored and sent

  • Google data travels from Google's servers to the app on your computer over HTTPS, and from your computer to your Car Thing over a local USB connection. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • The app keeps a copy of the most recently fetched events and tasks in its local settings file on your computer, so the Car Thing has something to show while offline. Disconnecting your Google account deletes it.
  • The Google refresh token, and any OAuth client secret you enter, are stored on your computer encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. Access tokens are kept only in memory.

Sharing

GlanceThing does not share, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. The developer has no access to it.

Other data

Sports scores are fetched from ESPN's public scoreboard without any personal information. Spotify features work as in the original GlanceThing and stay on your computer.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In the desktop app, open Settings → Google and click Disconnect. This revokes the app's access with Google and deletes the stored token and cached calendar data.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its settings folder removes everything it stored.

Limited Use

GlanceThing's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
